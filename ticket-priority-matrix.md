# Ticket Priority Matrix

Pick the priority from impact and urgency. When in doubt, go one level higher and adjust later.

| Priority | Impact | Examples | First response | Target fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 Critical** | Device can't sell or is unsafe; revenue or security at risk | Device offline, door won't lock, payments failing on every attempt | 15 min, any day | Same day |
| **P2 High** | Device works but key function is degraded | One shelf or sensor not detected, repeated session errors | 1 business hour | 1 to 2 business days |
| **P3 Normal** | Minor fault, workaround exists | One of two cameras offline, display glitch | Same business day | Next scheduled visit |
| **P4 Low** | Cosmetic or improvement | Messy cabling, signage, label requests | 2 business days | When convenient |

## Rules of thumb

- **Security beats everything.** A device that can be opened without paying is P1 even if it's selling fine.
- **Redundancy lowers priority.** If a backup component is still working, the failed one can wait for the next visit.
- **Repeat issues go up one level.** The second failure of the same part in 90 days is a pattern, not bad luck.
- **Out-of-hours P1s** notify the on-call person by text and email, not just the queue.
