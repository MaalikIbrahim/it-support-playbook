# Vendor Dispatch Template

Copy, fill in the brackets, send. Keep the ticket number in the subject so replies thread correctly.

---

**Subject:** Service request: [Device ID] at [Site name] ([Ticket #])

Hi [Vendor contact],

Could you please schedule a technician for the device below?

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Device | [Device ID / serial] |
| Site | [Site name, address, location in building] |
| Site contact | [Name, phone, hours] |
| Priority | [P1 to P4] |
| Our ticket | [Ticket #] |

**Problem:** [One or two sentences: what's wrong and since when.]

**Already tried remotely:** [Reboot, module restart, connectivity checks, results.]

**Likely cause:** [e.g. Shelf 2 USB cable or hub port; cable and hub already replaced on [date], so bring a spare shelf.]

**Parts to bring:** [List with part numbers.]

**On site:** please call [support phone] when you arrive so we can confirm the fix with you before you leave.

Could you reply with your ticket number and the visit date?

Thanks,
[Name]

---

## After the visit, record in the ticket

- Vendor ticket number and visit date
- Technician name
- What was found and what was replaced (part numbers)
- Test result before the tech left
- Faulty parts returned? Where to?
